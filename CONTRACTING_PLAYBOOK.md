## ⚡ 3. ADAPT: Bridging Research into Enterprise ROI

Enterprises are fatigued by "AI hype" and demo videos. They pay contractors to deliver tangible business outcomes.

### Translating "AGI" into Enterprise Language
- Instead of saying: *"I train multi-agent swarms using reinforcement learning."*
- Say: *"I build autonomous agent systems with strict deterministic guardrails that eliminate 80% of repetitive Tier-2 engineering tickets while preventing hallucinations."*

### Key Adaptation Principles
1. **The "Smallest Viable Agent" Rule**: Never build a complex 5-agent swarm when a single prompt with structured outputs and fallback logic does the job. Clients respect simplicity and cost efficiency.
2. **Deterministic Wrappers**: Wrap stochastic LLM outputs with rigorous Pydantic schemas, validation regexes, and retry loops.
3. **Observability First**: Before shipping agent behavior, instrument full tracing so executives can audit token spend, latency percentiles, and failure points.

## 🔒 4. SECURE: Commercial Structuring, Pricing & Retention

### Engagement Models
1. **The Architecture Audit & Discovery Sprint (Fixed Fee: £5,000 - £10,000 / 2 Weeks)**
   - Audit current AI stack, data pipelines, model latency, and failure logs.
   - Deliverable: Comprehensive Architecture Blueprint + Production Roadmap.
   - Low-friction entry point that naturally converts into long-term implementation.

2. **The Fractional / Retainer Contract (£8,000 - £16,000/month or £800 - £1,500/day)**
   - 2 to 4 days/week advisory and architectural steering.
   - Code reviews, team mentoring, model eval design, and direct client stakeholder meetings.

### Scope Creep Defense & Contract Safeguards
- **Clear Definition of Done (DoD)**: Specify acceptance criteria based on quantitative evals (e.g. *accuracy >= 92% on test suite of 500 prompts, p95 latency < 2.5s*) rather than subjective impressions.
- **Outside IR35 Compliance (UK)**:
  - Genuine business-to-business terms.
  - Right of substitution clause.
  - No mutuality of obligation (MOO).
  - Clear milestone deliverables.
- **Client Retention**: End every sprint with a 1-page "Impact & Next Horizon" memo highlighting cost savings achieved, latency reduced, and the next strategic capability to unlock.
