3. ADAPT: Bridging Research into Enterprise ROI

Translating theoretical AGI concepts into high-impact, deterministic business outcomes

💼 Translating "AGI" into Executive Language

  • Instead of: "I train multi-agent swarms using reinforcement learning."
  • Say: "I build autonomous agent systems with strict deterministic guardrails that eliminate 80% of repetitive Tier-2 engineering tickets while preventing hallucinations."
  • Focus on Unit Economics: Frame projects around reduced GPU token burn, lower response latency, and quantifiable FTE hours reclaimed.

🛡️ The "Smallest Viable Agent" Doctrine

  • Simplicity First: Never build a complex 5-agent swarm when a single prompt with structured outputs and fallback logic does the job.
  • Deterministic Wrappers: Shield non-deterministic models behind strict Pydantic schemas, validation regexes, and retry loops.
  • Observability First: Instrument full tracing before launch so CFOs and stakeholders can audit token spend and latency percentiles.

  • Quantitative Definition of Done (DoD): Bind deliverables to objective evals (e.g. accuracy ≥ 92% on test suite of 500 prompts, p95 latency < 2.5s) rather than subjective opinion.

  • Right of Substitution Clause: Clear contractual right to provide substitute personnel if necessary.
  • No Mutuality of Obligation (MOO): Project-bounded contract with explicit end dates and milestone deliverables.
